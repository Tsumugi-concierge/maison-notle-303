Route to accommodation
🗺 Google Maps
🚇 Step 1

Step 1: After exiting the ticket gate, turn left immediately and go up the escalator toward B1 Exit.

↩️ Step 2

Step 2: After exiting the station, turn left, then turn left again immediately.

⬆️ Step 3

Step 3: At the top of the escalator, turn left toward B1 Exit. If you continue straight, you can also use the elevator to street level. The elevator route is closer.

🚶 Step 4

Step 4: Walk through the passage toward B1 Exit and go up the stairs to street level. Once outside, turn left.
